01 // Geographic Distribution

The Internship Desert

Where are the jobs? Before unpacking the paradoxes, we need to understand where these opportunities actually exist — and who gets to reach them.

// AI Job Postings by Country — Top 20
📍 Geographic Concentration Jobs span 20+ countries, but the top 5 — Germany (814), Denmark (778), Canada (769), France (769), Austria (765) — account for roughly 26% of all listings. Students outside established tech economies face a structural disadvantage before sending a single application.
02 // The Experience Paradox

“Entry Level” Is a Lie

We filtered every listing tagged Entry Level (EN) and asked: how much prior experience do they actually require? The answer is quietly absurd.

// Number of Skills Required — “Entry Level” Roles
⚡ The Paradox Exposed The average “Entry Level” posting requires 0.49 years of experience — and demands between 3 and 5 discrete tools. Median is 4 skills. Some roles list Python, SQL, TensorFlow, Kubernetes, and Git simultaneously. For an internship.

Learning the Wrong Things

04 // The Skill Web

You Are Not Hired for One Skill

Skills in entry-level AI roles do not appear alone — they cluster in tight ecosystems. Asking for Python almost always means TensorFlow, Kubernetes, and Git come with it. Drag nodes to explore the connections.

🕸️ The Ecosystem Burden Python is the central hub — co-appearing with TensorFlow 310 times, Kubernetes 181 times, GCP 179 times, Git 177 times in entry-level roles alone. Hiring is no longer for a skill. It is an audition for an entire technology stack.
05 // The Verdict

A Structural Crisis

This is not about individual shortcomings. Four systemic failures are embedded into the AI job market itself.

🗺️

Geographic Bias

Opportunities cluster in established economies. Students elsewhere face a structural disadvantage before a single application is written.

🚫

The Zero-Year Myth

“Entry Level” is a misnomer. The market expects prior professional exposure for roles designed for beginners.

📚

Curriculum Blindspot

TensorFlow, Kubernetes, PyTorch — the most demanded AI skills are largely absent from standard academic training paths.

🕸️

The Ecosystem Burden

Interns are expected to navigate an entire interconnected tool stack — not simply demonstrate one language.
